How it works

  1. Start LANcelot

    Double-click on the classroom PC. The teacher screen opens on the board.

  2. Students scan

    They scan the QR code or type the address. Enter a name and done.

  3. Play and learn

    You pick the quiz and the game. The class plays along, you follow everything live.

Five halls, one way of working

Every part has an icon and one sentence of explanation. Know one hall and you know them all.

  • The Arena

    Practise through play with your own quizzes: quick quizzes, duels, team games and adventures.

  • The Round Table

    Ask the whole class: polls, statements, word clouds, open questions and circle time.

  • The Forge

    Make quizzes, worksheets and tests, with AI or by hand. Play them live or print them with an answer key.

  • The Armoury

    Classroom tools on the board: picker, groups, timer, noise meter, dice and class jobs.

  • My class

    Your class list, your country's curriculum goals or your own, tasks per pupil and the results.

40+ games for your own lessons

Every game plays with every quiz. Right answers give fuel, ammo, energy or a power. Answer well and you get further.

Lesson material in seconds, with AI

Give a topic and the grade, or drop in a photo of your textbook. The AI makes a quiz, a worksheet or a full set per lesson, on three levels.

  • Works with Gemini, ChatGPT or Claude: LANcelot copies the prompt, you paste the answer back.
  • Or enter your own key and everything happens in one click.
  • Play worksheets live in class or print them with an answer key.
  • Only creating with AI needs the internet. Play runs over the classroom network.

Every student at their own level

Per subject you choose a level for each student. Quizzes, worksheets and tasks adapt.

  1. Page

    Extra support and easier questions

  2. Squire

    The regular content

  3. Knight

    A challenge for those who can go further

Follow-up, support and reports

Every answer goes into the log, linked to the goals you choose. You see at once who needs extra help.

  • Support overview

    Students who need the most attention are at the top. See which goals the whole class should revisit, who needs small-group instruction and which mistakes keep coming back.

  • Tasks per student

    Assign a quiz or worksheet to one student or a small group, each at their own level. You can prepare it at home too.

  • Goals for your country

    Choose the curriculum goals of England, the US, the Netherlands, Germany, France or Flanders, or add your own. An AI can suggest them. Working without goals is fine too.

  • Tests with answer sheet

    Make a test in the Forge, let pupils take it online or print it. The scores go straight into your overview.

Student data stays in your classroom

  • LANcelot runs on your own PC. No cloud and no student accounts.
  • Names, answers and results live in a folder on that PC. You decide what happens to them.
  • No ads and no tracking.
  • If you use AI, only your prompt (topic or lesson material) goes to the AI you choose. No student names.

Frequently asked questions

When can I use LANcelot?

LANcelot is coming soon to the Microsoft Store, with a trial month in which everything works. This website is the first place to hear when it is ready.

What does LANcelot cost?

A one-time €29.99, yours to keep. First you try everything for a month without paying. No subscription and no cost per pupil.

Does LANcelot work with my country's goals?

Yes. In My class, choose your country's curriculum goals, add your own, or work without goals. Every answer then lands under the right goal in your overview.

Do I need the internet?

Play runs over the school's local network: the PC and the students' devices must be on the same network. Only creating lesson material with AI needs the internet.

Do students need an account?

No. They type their name and play. LANcelot remembers their knight and results on the classroom PC.

How many students can play?

There is no limit. A whole class of thirty plays smoothly, on Chromebooks, tablets and phones mixed.

What do I need?

A Windows PC (usually the one connected to the board), the school network and devices with a browser for the students.

Can I use my own lesson content?

Of course. Write quizzes yourself, let the AI make them from your own textbook or PDF, and sort everything into folders per subject and lesson.
